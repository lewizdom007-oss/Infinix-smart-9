## Packaging caution

The reference `naden01/infinix_X6532:ofox` BoardConfig declares `BOARD_DTB_SIZE := 161454`, which differs from the extracted FDT payload by 2,213 bytes. Do not silently copy this value or assume that changing it to 163667 is sufficient. Confirm how the selected TWRP branch's mkbootimg packs the DTB into vendor_boot v4 and validate the resulting image with unpacking tools.

The preliminary DTS is generated by a heuristic parser and has not been round-trip validated with `dtc`. Treat it as a reference, not a source of truth.

## Build policy

- Keep the stock kernel 5.10 for the first bootable recovery attempt.
- Target `vendor_boot.img`, not a legacy recovery partition.
- Do not flash an artifact until its header/layout, size, DTB/DTBO handling, and rollback method have been checked.
